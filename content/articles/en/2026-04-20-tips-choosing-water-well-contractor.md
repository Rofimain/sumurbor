## Why Contractor Selection Matters

A water well is a long-term investment. Choosing the wrong contractor can lead
to poor water quality, unstable yield, or even rapid well failure.

## 5 Practical Tips

### 1. Check Legal Permits and Certifications

Make sure the contractor holds valid SBU, NIB, and legal documents.
For commercial projects, HSE certification matters too.

### 2. Insist on a Site Survey

Good contractors offer a geoelectric survey before drilling — not just
drilling at a random spot.

### 3. Ask for Sample Bore Logs

Bore logs prove professionalism. Reputable contractors always document
soil conditions per depth.

### 4. Ensure a Pumping Test

A yield test after drilling is mandatory to confirm well capacity matches
your needs.

### 5. Confirm Workmanship Warranty

Minimum 6-month workmanship warranty. Confident vendors will not hesitate
to provide it in writing.
