## Bored Pile Service

Bored pile is a deep foundation type formed by drilling soil to a defined depth
then filling it with reinforcement and concrete. Suited for projects with heavy
loads or sites sensitive to vibration.

### Best for

- High-rise buildings and apartments
- Bridges and flyovers
- Factories and warehouses with heavy loads
- Telecom and transmission towers

### Advantages

- **Minimal vibration** — safe for neighboring structures
- **High bearing capacity** — fits heavy-load structures
- **Flexible diameter** and depth tailored to needs
- **Quality controlled** with slump test and PIT
