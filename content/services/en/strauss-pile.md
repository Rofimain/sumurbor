## Strauss Pile Service

Strauss pile is an economical manual auger foundation technique well-suited
to sites with limited access — narrow alleys or renovations. Fast installation
at a lower cost than mechanized bored pile.

### Best for

- 1–3 story houses
- Small shops and kiosks
- Renovations or structural additions
- Sites that cannot be reached by heavy equipment
