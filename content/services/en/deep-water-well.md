## Deep Water Well Service

Deep water wells are the solution for consistent, high-volume clean water,
especially in areas where shallow groundwater is insufficient or low quality.

### Best for

- Manufacturing plants
- Hotels, hospitals, and apartments
- Cluster housing
- Farms and plantations

### Workflow

1. **Site survey and geoelectric** to identify the optimal drilling point
2. **Rig mobilization** matched to target depth
3. **Drilling** with per-meter borehole logging
4. **Casing and screen installation** with gravel pack
5. **Pumping test** for yield measurement and stabilization
6. **Water quality testing** to applicable standards
7. **Pump installation** and commissioning
