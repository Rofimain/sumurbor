## Project Summary

Bored pile foundation for a 14-story residential tower in BSD, South Tangerang.
Completed in 45 days using a combination of hydraulic and rotary rigs, on schedule.

## Challenges

- Site sandwiched between existing buildings → low-vibration required
- Soft soil in the top 8 m
- Tight 45 calendar-day schedule

## Approach

- Temporary casing for borehole stability
- Slump test on every concrete batch
- 2-shift crew with daily documentation
- 100% PIT on all piles
