## Project Summary

A 120 m deep water well for a manufacturing plant in Cikarang, Bekasi.
Pumping test confirmed 3 L/s yield with water quality meeting industrial standards.

## Scope

- 1-point geoelectric survey
- 120 m drilling with 8″ casing
- 48-hour continuous pumping test
- Accredited lab water quality test
- 7.5 kW submersible pump installation
